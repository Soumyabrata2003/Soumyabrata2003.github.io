We release ProForma-20Q, a reproducible benchmark for forecasting 78 financial statement line items 1 to 20 quarters ahead, and Forma, a tuple set transformer that reads statements as sets of (account, quarter, value) tuples and maximizes a masked tuple Gaussian likelihood. Forma beats classical machine learning, chained gradient boosting, a zero shot time series foundation model, and frontier LLMs, with its advantage widening at the long horizons that matter most for valuation.
